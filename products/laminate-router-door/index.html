Laminate Router Doors

Our Laminate Router Doors offer an elegant and stylish look with a wide range of 2D designs to complement modern interiors. Made from premium-quality Sanmica laminate sheets and a durable flush door base, these doors are strong, long-lasting, and low-maintenance. Available in a variety of colours, patterns, and designs, they are a perfect choice for adding a beautiful and modern touch to both homes and commercial spaces.

  • Premium Sanmica Laminate Finish
  • Stylish 2D Router Designs
  • Strong & Durable Flush Door Base
  • Available in Various Colours & Design
  • Suitable for Homes & Commercial Spaces
